# Billed line adjustments and receipt credit attribution

This is the next bounded A3/A4 slice of the [existing delivery gates](procurement-costing-remaining-delivery.md). It uses existing invoice review, supplier credit, source revision, receipt interval and inventory application owners. It adds no monetary table or parallel posting engine.

## What the code does

`invoiceCostAdjustmentSchema` records an adjustment already included in one invoice line: stable reference, supplier label, exact signed mills, category and explicit destination (product, packaging or outside inventory). Tax retains its configured tax owner. `invoiceGoodsAmounts` selects net goods without rewriting the bill or treating a credit note as an invoice edit. `resolveLegacyPurchaseCostSource` requires product + packaging + billed adjustments to equal the unchanged line total; configured tax plus classified non-tax amounts must account for every adjustment. Missing classifications retain review. A separate packaging charge cannot be added when packaging is included in product.

`reviewInvoiceCostComponentsInTransaction` retains the existing Administrator/purchasing approval, rollback-only preview, mandatory measured version, durable retry and immutable linked PO approval. The UI preserves saved classifications on reopening. Invoice correction and tax review carry those classifications forward and compare the complete reviewed intent; changing the tax does not discard a saved discount.

`supplierCreditDispositionSchema` allows a price reduction to name an exact **saved invoice-to-receipt assignment**. `readSupplierCreditSourceOptions` exposes only completed, saved assignments; the create form requires the billed receipt. `validateSupplierCreditSourceLines` rechecks that same assignment under the existing graph transaction lock. Source evidence freezes it alongside the original invoice fingerprint. Whole-invoice and per-receipt component caps reject cumulative over-crediting. The original invoice, supplier credit document, settlement applications and physical return remain separate owners.

`resolveReceiptInvoiceSources` no longer rejects all supplier credits. It validates active price credits, maps them only to their frozen receipt interval and adds their source references. The exact gross receipt share is reduced once, then `receiptSegmentCost` allocates that net amount over the same pieces. It does not subtract independently rounded credits from every stock unit. Other receipts retain their own invoice prices; uncovered pieces remain provisional. A void retains the original credit evidence and restores the gross receipt cost through the same source/application owners. Accepted-stock returns continue through their existing independent outgoing-value owner; unsupported quarantine-return combinations retain review.

Receipt-attributed credit approve, void and cost recheck run through `captureSupplierCreditCostReview` / `finishSupplierCreditCostReview` around `executeSupplierCreditCommand`. The real owner executes in a rollback-only preview. Approval binds the exact current document/dependencies, persisted Administrator and purchasing approval, reason, before/after stock valuation, order cost basis and existing historical destinations. The review leads with receipt numbers, pieces and supplier cost before/after from the shared source projection; stock/order details remain expandable. A stale preview rejects and rolls back. The durable command key makes lost-response retry replay the original result. Immutable `supplier_credit_costs_approved` PO events retain actor/name, time, reason and measured effect. The credit detail renders those original approvals and reversals.

Forward migration `900000052000019930001_receipt_supplier_credit_sources.sql` extends the existing `guard_supplier_credit_history` owner. The source key distinguishes receipts on one note while preserving legacy key bytes. The database checks the exact saved assignment and frozen evidence on insertion, locks its source rows, and rechecks membership at approval. Existing document conservation, settlement capacity, reversal and immutable-history checks remain. The migration changes no existing row. Real migration tests cover old keys, malformed/unsafe attribution, mismatched saved assignments, immutable lines and invalidated membership at approval; the full legacy credit HTTP/settlement suite also runs under the extended guard.

## Concrete example

Two receipt batches have $25 and $30 in supplier goods costs. On the first bill, the supplier confirms $26 product, a $2 product discount and a $1 service outside inventory: **bill $25 unchanged; first receipt inventory $24; second receipt $30 unchanged**. A separate $2 credit explicitly belonging to the first receipt yields **$22 and $30**. Voiding that credit restores **$24 and $30**, retaining both approval records. Received units, original bill totals, cash, the original receipt-assignment approval and the second receipt do not change. This is the connected desktop/mobile acceptance scenario.

## Deliberate boundaries and remaining work

No product/packaging split, receipt, sale, expense destination, FX rate or header allocation is inferred. A known combined amount without its split, unapportioned invoice/PO header adjustments, tax-credit attribution and additional receipt/quarantine-return combinations remain A3/A4 gates. This slice supports classified amounts already belonging to a billed line and explicitly attributed price credits; it does not declare all A3/A4 cases complete. Supplier freight entered here must actually belong to that billed line and must not also be recorded as a shipment charge; the system cannot prove two separately entered references describe the same real-world service without source evidence.

Existing legacy credit contracts/retries remain readable. New receipt-attributed credit posting needs the measured admin approval. Old immutable source revisions and approval payloads are not migrated or rewritten. Exact known amounts still do not prove external accounting expense recognition; the downstream recognition gate remains separate.

Validation results and exact PR/head acceptance are recorded in the main tracker and PR. Real PostgreSQL acceptance covers actual receiving/assignment, exact adjustment conservation, receipt-only credit application, unchanged physical/bill/cash facts, original approval retention, reversal/replay, stale source, permission revocation, target mismatch, cumulative caps, audit failure rollback/retry and concurrent approval. A transferred-receipt case verifies stock/order cost propagation and zero invented historical expense postings. Unit coverage includes invalid and unsafe values, malformed segment coverage/identities, net rounding, source withdrawal, accepted-return independence and unchanged compatibility. Connected desktop/mobile exercises the actual forms, HTTP owners, SQL values and retained approval display.
